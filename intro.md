# Husni Sarafi — Intro

I'm an RPA Consultant based in Kuala Lumpur. By day, I build and maintain enterprise automation systems — designing workflows that integrate databases, APIs, and ERP systems to eliminate manual bottlenecks. Currently at EY, assigned to a TNB engagement handling BluePrism processes across SMART Meter Billing, AMI, and other utility operations.

But automation is just my day job. What actually drives me is the space where automation meets AI.

On my own time, I build things that scratch my own itches:

- **Meeting Min** — a self-hosted meeting transcription system that captures audio from Teams meetings, pipes it through Whisper for speech-to-text, and generates bilingual (Malay/English) summaries using LLMs. Designed for the reality of corporate environments where you don't have admin access to APIs — so it captures audio locally and processes in the cloud via SSH.

- **Mazda AIO** — a custom infotainment project for my 2015 Mazda 2. I've built speedometer dashboards and instrument cluster UIs in HTML/JS that run on the car's CMU head unit. Part aesthetic, part wanting to understand embedded systems and reverse engineering.

- **Yeelight Backlight** — a Python script that turns a Yeelight bslamp3 into a monitor ambient light. It captures screen edges in real-time, averages colors, and sends commands to the bulb over LAN. Iterated through 6 versions — dealing with TCP buffer overflow, color smoothing, dark mode fallback, and rate limiting along the way.

- **Celestial Clock** — a full-screen canvas application that renders a prayer-synced sky (gradient tied to actual Fajr/Sunrise/Maghrib times via Aladhan API), sun arc, moon phases, and mountains. Was in the middle of a full rewrite toward a calmer, more minimal design.

I think in systems. I gravitate toward tools that give me control — self-hosting over SaaS, local LLMs (Ollama) over cloud-only APIs, CLIs over GUIs. I use WSL on Windows because I need both worlds. My Obsidian vault is my second brain, restructured from PARA to a flat project-based workspace because I'd rather get work done than maintain a filing system.

Background-wise, I have a degree in Actuarial Science from UiTM Shah Alam. The math and probability thinking carries over into everything I do — whether I'm modeling trading systems (Bursa sector catalyst model, currently stalled) or debugging why a bulb isn't responding to TCP commands at 2am.

Currently exploring AI engineering as the next move. Running Hermes as my daily AI assistant, experimenting with local models, and figuring out how to bridge the gap between traditional RPA and intelligent automation. The boring parts of the job fund the interesting ones.
